## Case Study 1 - Hilda’s Haberdashery
They sell a wide variety of products including
(but not limited to):
• Dressmaking Fabrics
• Curtaining Fabrics
• Sewing Machines
• Wool
• Knitting Needles
• Crochet Kits
• Cross Stitch Kits
• Embroidery Kits
• Embroidery Threads
• Aida
Hilda’s Haberdashery have employed DTD to develop them a new stock management system for their
customers. As a result of the pandemic, Hilda has seen a sharp rise in sales due to an increase in people
using sewing, knitting, and other crafts to support their mental health and wellbeing. As a result, their
current stock management system is no longer fit for purpose. They have identified the following key
pieces of functionality are required for their new system:
• Product Listings
• Search
• Low Stock Alerts
• Stock Value Output
• Reporting
• Add New Products
• Delist Products
• Change Stock Levels (i.e. reduce and increase)
They are, however, open to other suggestions in relation to features which should or could be added to
their system
